Guimaras Island: A Tropical Paradise

Nestled in the heart of the Philippines, Guimaras Island is a hidden gem that welcomes travelers seeking a tropical paradise. The island is known for its beautiful beaches, green landscapes, peaceful atmosphere, and warm hospitality.

Visitors can explore white sand beaches, island resorts, historical landmarks, and famous mango plantations. Guimaras is also known for its colorful Manggahan Festival celebrated every year.

Images for the Home Page

Guimaras Island: An Island-Hopping Adventure

Guimaras offers exciting island-hopping adventures with crystal-clear waters and beautiful beaches. Tourists can visit Alubihod Beach, Guisi Lighthouse, Turtle Island Marine Reserve, and many hidden island destinations.

Visitors can enjoy swimming, snorkeling, sightseeing, and relaxing while experiencing the peaceful beauty of Guimaras.

Images for the About Guimaras Page

Guimaras: Home of the Manggahan Festival

Guimaras is famous for producing some of the sweetest mangoes in the world. The island celebrates the annual Manggahan Festival to honor its rich mango harvest, culture, traditions, and local products.

During the festival, visitors can enjoy street dancing, cultural presentations, local delicacies, mango-eating contests, and many colorful events.
