Guimaras, Philippines

Guimaras is an island province in the Philippines with a history rooted in early Austronesian settlements that relied on fishing, farming, and trade with nearby islands. Today, it is known for its beautiful beaches, vibrant culture, and sweet mangoes, making it a popular destination for tourists. Known as the “Mango Capital of the Philippines,” it celebrates its heritage through the Manggahan Festival.
